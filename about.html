Our Story

The Founder

Afaq Ahmad

With over 15 years in international luxury real estate, Afaq Ahmad founded the firm on a simple principle: every client deserves the same level of care, discretion, and expertise that the world's most discerning investors expect.

From a single office to a global advisory network, the journey has been defined by relationships — not transactions. Today, Afaq Ahmad Real Estate operates across six continents, serving clients who value quality over quantity.

"Real estate is not about buildings. It's about the lives that unfold within them."

— Afaq Ahmad
